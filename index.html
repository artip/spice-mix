How to Play

Turn BEEF into BEET — change one letter per step. Press Enter to lock in each word.

BEEF Start
BEES F → S
BEET Target!
Green border — valid move locked in
Red + shake — invalid word or too many changes
↩ ×1 — one undo per puzzle to take back your last word

Two puzzles per day — Easy (4 letters) & Hard (5 letters). Both reset at midnight.
